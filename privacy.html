In short

Farm & Fence is a puzzle game that plays offline. The app does not collect any personal data: there is no user account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no cookies and no server of our own. We do not learn who plays the game.

2. Game progress and settings

Your solved puzzles, your farm, your settings and the information which chapters are unlocked are stored only on your device. They are not transmitted to us.

If backups are turned on for your device (iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad, Google backup on Android), your progress may be part of that backup and can be moved to a new device. Apple and Google perform these backups under their own privacy policies; we have no access to them.

You can delete your progress at any time in the app under Settings → “Delete progress”. Deleting the app also deletes the data stored on the device.

3. In-app purchases

Additional chapters can be bought through Apple’s App Store or Google Play. Payment and billing are handled exclusively by Apple or Google. We do not receive any payment details, names or email addresses.

To unlock purchased content and restore it on a new device, the app asks the store which items were bought with your store account. This information is used only on the device. On iPhone and iPad the app uses Apple’s purchase interface (StoreKit). On Android, Google’s “Google Play Billing Library” is included in the app for this purpose and connects to Google Play. The app only needs an internet connection for purchases and restoring them, not for the game.

Apple and Google process purchase and store account data as independent controllers under their own privacy policies: Apple and Google.

The gift after 200 solved puzzles is not a purchase. It is only recorded in your progress on the device.

4. Crash reports and statistics

The app contains no crash reporting or usage analytics tools of its own. Apple and Google provide app developers with aggregated statistics and crash reports if you have agreed to this in your device settings (for example “Share With App Developers” on iPhone and iPad). We receive this information without names or contact details and cannot link it to a person.

5. Permissions

Farm & Fence does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or any other personal content on your device.

6. Contacting us by email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer your request and delete them once they are no longer needed and no legal retention period applies. Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR (answering your request).

7. This website

These pages are provided through GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you visit them, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data such as your device’s IP address, for example to deliver the pages and to protect the service. This may involve a transfer of data to the USA. Details are in GitHub’s privacy statement. We ourselves use no cookies, no scripts, no fonts from third-party servers and no analytics or tracking tools on these pages. Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure, simple website).

8. Children

Farm & Fence contains no advertising and collects no personal data – including from children.

9. Your rights

Under the FADP and, where applicable, the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Since the app does not send any data to us, this usually only concerns emails you have sent us. Please contact us at the address above.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority – in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the authority of your country of residence.

10. Changes

We update this privacy policy when the app or the legal situation changes. The version published here applies.
